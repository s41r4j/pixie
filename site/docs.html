Install

Download the .dmg, drag Pixie to Applications, then right-click → Open the first time. (A Windows preview .exe is on the Download page.) The :) appears in the menu bar and the welcome window starts: your name, permissions, a model key, a mic test, and the shortcuts. The natural voice downloads in the background while you go.

Screen Recording needs a relaunch after you allow it. If a switch is on but Pixie says "not allowed", use Reset in Settings → Permissions.

Models and keys

Settings → Models: pick a provider, paste its key. Models load live from the provider, newest first, with context size and price. Mark one for talking (T), one for agents (A), and optionally one fallback that answers if the main model fails.

The buddy

The round :) rides just off your pointer. It points at real controls by name, draws circles, underlines and selections, and clicks or types when you ask. Settings → General: Always on, Idle (only while Pixie is busy) or Off; and whether it may click for you.

Agents

Background agents work on their own (research, documents, files, code) and keep a live note. Live agents do the task on your screen and explain each step. Both show in the notch's Agents tab; past jobs and chats are in the Jobs window. Agents never overwrite your files and can't touch .ssh, .aws, keychains or .env files.

Apps and accounts

Settings → Apps switches on Calendar, Reminders, Contacts and Apple Mail; each asks macOS once. Then just ask: “what's on tomorrow?”, “remind me to call Sam at 5”, “draft a reply to Alex”. Drafts open for you to send; Pixie never sends mail.

Google: create an OAuth client of type Desktop app in Google Cloud, enable the Gmail and Calendar APIs, paste the client ID and secret, and sign in. Pixie reads Gmail, saves drafts and reads or adds events; tokens stay on your Mac.

Connectors

Settings → Connectors adds GitHub, Notion, Slack, Linear, Jira & Confluence, Brave Search or a folder in one click (paste a token where one is needed; Linear and Atlassian sign in in your browser). They run through Node.js. Any other MCP server goes in mcp.json.

Voice and sounds

The natural voice runs offline (about 370 MB, downloaded during setup); the system voice works meanwhile. Settings → Sounds lets you change, quieten or replace every sound with your own file.

Privacy

Speech recognition, screen OCR and the natural voice run on your Mac. Screenshots and your words go only to the model provider you chose, with your key. Usage is counted locally. There is no Pixie server.

Troubleshooting

"Can't be opened": right-click → Open, or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Permission not sticking: Settings → Permissions → Reset, allow, relaunch. No answer: check the key in Settings → Models. Still stuck? Open an issue.
