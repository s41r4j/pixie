Talk, don't type

Hold ⌃⌥, ask, let go. Speech is recognised on your Mac and the answer is spoken back. No text over your work.

Watch →
02 / BUDDY

Points and draws

A round :) rides beside your cursor, flies to the exact button and circles, underlines or selects it.

Watch →
