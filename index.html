features

Like a friend at your elbow, with a pointer.

No chat window to switch to. It answers out loud, shows you on the screen, does the clicking when you ask, and gets out of the way.

Talk, don't type

Hold control and option, ask, let go. A natural voice answers a second later, offline on your Mac.

Its own pointer

Pixie rides beside your cursor, flies to the thing it's talking about and labels it, like someone pointing over your shoulder.

Clicks for you

"Do it for me": it clicks, types and drags one step at a time, checking the screen after each. Or keep it to pointing only.

Explains on a board

Ask how something works and it sketches it on a clean board while it talks you through it.

Codes with your agents

Bigger jobs hand code to Codex CLI, Claude Code or opencode on your Mac, then check the result.

Private by default

Speech and voice run on your Mac. Screens and files only leave it when a question needs a model.

Hover the notch

Talk, type, your running jobs and the last answer, one glance away.

A board for explaining

Diagrams get their own paper, so nothing covers what it's drawing.

Notes, not chat

Every job writes a page: the answer first, then details, with buttons for what's next.

Setup that helps

When macOS needs a switch flipped, Pixie hands you a tile to drag straight into the list.

setup

Running in two minutes.

Download

Open the DMG and drag Pixie to Applications. It lives in your menu bar, not your Dock.

Four switches

Microphone, speech, screen and accessibility. Pixie walks you through each and hands you a tile to drag.

Add your key

Paste a Claude or DeepSeek key. You pay your provider directly; Pixie costs nothing.

git clone https://github.com/s41r4j/pixie && cd pixie && npm install && npm run app:build && npm run app:install
